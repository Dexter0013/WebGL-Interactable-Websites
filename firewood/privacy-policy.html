Privacy Policy

Firewood Splitting Simulator has no ads or developer-operated servers, and we don’t sell your data or track you for advertising. There’s no separate Firewood account. Your settings and progress are saved on your device, with cloud syncing through Apple or Google when available.

The app uses Apple Game Center and iCloud on iOS, and Google Play Games on Android, for sign-in, leaderboards, achievements, and cloud saves. These services process gaming profiles, scores, achievements, saved progress, and identifiers needed for syncing. Google’s Play Games SDK also collects diagnostics and analytics to improve its services. The Google Play Data safety disclosures reflect these standard integrations.

Optional purchases are handled by Apple or Google. The app checks purchase information to unlock or restore Full Access; we do not receive your payment details. These services handle information according to Apple’s Privacy Policy and Google’s Privacy Policy.

Because we do not collect or store your personal game data on developer-operated servers, there is no such data for us to retain or delete. Data stored by Apple or Google can be managed or deleted through their account and privacy controls.
